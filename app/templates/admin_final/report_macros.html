How to read this report
Requested
Every line departments have submitted, at the amount they asked for. In Review, Approved, Reduced and Rejected add up to it. Drafts are not included.
In Review
Lines waiting for dispatch, under reviewer review, or paused behind a primary request. A reviewer's approval or rejection is a recommendation, so those lines stay here until the budget admin decides them at admin final.
Approved
The amount set at admin final. A line counts here as soon as the budget admin approves it, before the request is finalized. Approved is not released: departments are told only after the board approves the event budget.
Reduced
The difference between what was requested and what was approved, on approved lines. It is negative when the budget admin approved more than the request.
Rejected
Lines rejected at admin final, plus reviewer rejections that stood when the request was finalized.
Current Ceiling
Approved plus everything still in review at its requested amount. It drops as lines in review are reduced or rejected. It rises only when new requests are submitted or the budget admin approves more than a line requested.
{% if show_draft %}
Draft
Lines on requests the department has not submitted. They are not part of Requested.
{% else %}
Unsubmitted drafts
A department holding a draft request shows a badge with the count and amount. Drafts are not part of Requested, so check the badge for departments that may have forgotten to submit.
{% endif %}
