# Stack a pallet

Stacking tells the system where totes and pallets are now. A lead or the pallet jack driver does it.

1. Scan the pallet. The screen says "Going to" and names the pallet.
2. Scan each tote as you add it. The screen confirms each move.
3. Tap **New destination** for the next pallet. The screen asks where it is going.
4. Scan the next pallet's label to continue.

## A fresh label

Scan a label the system has not seen. The screen says it is a new label and where it goes. Tap the kind that matches what you are labelling. A new tote lands straight on the destination.

## Move a case of paper

1. Tap **Move supplies** while a pallet is showing. The screen says "Scan where they are coming from."
2. Scan the pallet or tote the paper is on. The screen lists items.
3. Tap the item. The number pad opens.
4. Tap **Packs of** if the item is counted in cases, then enter the number and tap **OK**. The paper moves to the pallet you are stacking.

## A wrong move

There is no undo. For totes and assets, tap **New destination**, scan the right place, then scan the thing again. If loose items moved by mistake, move them back with **Move supplies**.
